FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you only take on selected clients?

Critical illness planning requires deep, personalized attention and continuous monitoring. By limiting my clientele, I ensure that every client receives white-glove service, especially during the critical moments of a claim.

What happens during the initial qualification call?

It's a relaxed, 15-minute conversation where we discuss your financial goals and health protection priorities. There is no pressure or obligation; it's simply to see if our approaches align.

Do you only handle critical illness coverage?

While my specialty and primary focus is critical illness and income protection, I provide holistic planning for my exclusive clients, including life and medical coverage, to ensure their entire portfolio is robust.
